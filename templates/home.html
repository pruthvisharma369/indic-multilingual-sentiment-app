About the Project

Why sentiment analysis needs a multilingual lens

Most sentiment models are trained almost exclusively on English text, leaving out the hundreds of millions of people who write and speak in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, and India's many other languages.

This project fine-tunes IndicBERT — a transformer pretrained on a large corpus of Indian-language text — to classify sentence-level sentiment as Positive or Negative across 12 languages, without relying on machine translation as an intermediate step.
